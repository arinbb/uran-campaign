Submit a result

After you fly, IL-2 writes a text log of exactly what happened — who took off, who was shot down, whether the objective was met. That log is the only thing the campaign trusts; nobody has to self-report a win. Find it under your game's data/logs/ folder (typically Documents\\1C SoftClub\\il-2 sturmovik great battles\\data\\logs\\ or similar depending on your install), as one or more files named missionReport(<timestamp>)[N].txt from the session you just flew.

This opens a new GitHub issue in this repo with your log text attached, using your own GitHub login — nothing is uploaded to a server. Review it and click "Submit new issue." The next weekly turn resolution reads it from there.
